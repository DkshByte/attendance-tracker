10:58

Ten seconds in a corridor.

Between classes you have one hand and no time. Open Bunkr and the answer is already on screen.

  1. The class you're in, and when it ends.

    A plate at the top counts down the period. Under it, what's next, who takes it, and when.

  2. Your whole week as one line.

    Every day drawn as stations: lectures, labs, lunch and free periods at their real length. Tap a day to ride its line.

  3. Which days it shuts, and what that costs.

    Holidays come with the classes they take out, so a long weekend never surprises your percentage.

13:20

Then you sit down and decide.

You need 75% in every subject, not 75% overall. A spare class in one subject can't cover a missed one in another. Bunkr counts each subject against every class left in the term, and tells you the consequence, not just the number.
