Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 September 2026 · Applies to bunkr.website and the Bunkr Android app

The short version

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies. We never sell your data.
  • Your attendance marks are private to your account.
  • Signing in with Google is optional; Google only shares your email, name and picture.
  • Your name, username, batch and rank appear on your section's leaderboard.
  • Contact details you choose to add are visible to signed-in classmates.
  • You can clear your marks in the app, and ask us to delete your account at any time.

1. Who runs Bunkr

Bunkr is an attendance tracker and timetable built and run by Daksh Sharma, an individual developer in New Delhi, India ("we", "us"). For your data, we are the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Questions go to support@bunkr.website.

3. Who can see what

  • Only you: your attendance marks, your email, your device details.
  • Signed-in classmates in Bunkr: your name, username, batch, role, and any contact details you chose to add, in the section directory.
  • Anyone who uses Bunkr, signed in or not: the section leaderboard (name, username, batch and rank), which roster names have been claimed, and the section's cancelled classes and holidays.
  • Us: we can access the database to run and fix the service. We do not look at individual records unless you ask for help or we have to investigate abuse or a fault.

We share personal data only with the service providers below, or if the law requires it.

5. Cookies and on-device storage

Bunkr sets no cookies at all: no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and no third-party trackers. Our fonts and files are served from bunkr.website itself, so opening a page does not send your details to anyone else. The one exception is your choice: tapping Continue with Google takes you to Google's own sign-in page, where Google's cookies and policy apply. Because nothing non-essential is stored and nothing is shared for tracking, there is nothing to ask your permission for, and so no cookie banner.

To work, and to keep working with no signal, Bunkr stores these in your browser's local storage or the app's storage on your device:

You can remove it at any time through your browser's site settings or by uninstalling the app; you will be signed out and offline data will be gone. If we ever add anything that is not strictly necessary, such as analytics, we will ask for your permission first and update this page.

6. On your phone

  • Notifications: reminders to mark attendance are scheduled on your phone. No notification server receives your data.
  • Fingerprint or face lock: if you turn on app lock, Android checks your biometrics. Bunkr only learns whether the check passed and never receives fingerprint or face data.
  • Updates: the app downloads the latest version of Bunkr from bunkr.website when it opens.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account, roster link, profile and attendance: while your account exists. When you delete your account we erase them within 30 days.
  • Attendance marks you clear with "Clear all records" are removed from your account straight away.
  • Leaderboard totals are recalculated from your marks and disappear with your account.
  • Provider logs are kept for the periods set by Supabase and Vercel for security and operations.
  • At the end of an academic session we may reset the section's data for the next term; we will tell users in the app before we do.

8. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or update it (most of it you can edit in your profile);
  • have it erased, and withdraw your consent, by deleting your account;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act;
  • raise a grievance with us, and if we do not resolve it, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Email support@bunkr.website from the address on your account. We reply within 30 days. If you are in another country, you may have similar rights under your local law and can use the same address.

9. Delete your account

Send a deletion request from the email address you signed up with, and include your Bunkr username.

Request account deletion

Within 30 days we delete your sign-in account, your roster link and profile (contact details included), every attendance mark, your leaderboard entry, your device record and your cancelled-class reports, and release your roster name so it can be claimed again. Holidays, notices or class changes you posted as a CR stay for the section, without your name attached. Data on your own device is removed when you sign out, clear site data or uninstall.

You can clear all your attendance marks yourself at any time: Attendance → Clear all records.

10. If you are under 18

Bunkr is meant for college students. If you are under 18, only use Bunkr with the permission of a parent or guardian, and they may contact us to review or delete your data. We do not knowingly track, profile or target anyone under 18.

11. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Database access rules make sure an account can only read and change its own private records. Passwords are hashed by our sign-in provider. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and its date, and tell you in the app before a significant change takes effect.
