Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026 · Applies to bunkr.website and the Bunkr Android app

The short version

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies. We never sell or rent your data, and we do not use it to profile you.
  • Your attendance marks, email address, enrolment number and device details are private to your account.
  • The section roster (names, serial numbers and batches) can be seen by anyone who opens Bunkr. If your name is on it and you want it removed, tell us.
  • Signing in with Google is optional; Google only shares your email, name and picture.
  • The leaderboard is off until you switch it on. Contact details are shown only if you add them, and only to signed-in classmates.
  • You can see, correct or delete your data, and withdraw your consent, at any time. Deleting your account is one email.
  • If you are under 18, a parent or guardian has to agree before you use Bunkr.

1. Who runs Bunkr and which laws apply

Bunkr is an attendance tracker and timetable built and run by Daksh Sharma, an individual developer in New Delhi, India ("we", "us"). Bunkr is an independent student project. It is not run by, connected to or endorsed by any college or university.

We decide why and how your personal data is used, so we are the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. Those provisions come into force in stages, with the main duties applying from 13 May 2027; we follow them now. Until then, section 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 also apply, and this policy is our privacy policy under those rules. Because classmates can see some of what you post, we also follow the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.

This policy is a notice in plain language. It stands by itself: you do not need to read anything else to understand what we do with your data. Questions go to support@bunkr.website. If you need this policy in Hindi or another Indian language, write to us and we will provide it.

4. If your name is on the roster but you have not joined

To let each student find and claim their own seat, Bunkr holds the section's class list: every student's full name, serial number and batch, and, where the list includes it, the university enrolment number. The list is loaded by us or by the section's class representative. Your name, serial number, batch and whether your seat has been claimed can be seen by anyone who opens Bunkr. Your enrolment number is not shown to anyone, and becomes visible only to the account that claims your seat.

You can have your entry removed. Email support@bunkr.website with your name and section. You do not need a Bunkr account. We will remove your name, serial number, batch and enrolment number within 15 days and confirm when it is done.

If someone else has claimed your seat, tell us at the same address. We will release it, remove anything they added and, where needed, close their account.

Until you claim your seat we hold nothing else about you, and we do not contact you. A classmate may send you an invitation link that contains your first name; that message is sent by them, from their own WhatsApp or other app, not by us.

8. On your phone

  • Notifications: reminders to mark attendance are scheduled on your phone. No notification server receives your data. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Fingerprint or face lock: if you turn on app lock, Android checks your biometrics. Bunkr only learns whether the check passed and never receives fingerprint or face data.
  • Updates: the app downloads the latest version of Bunkr from bunkr.website when it opens.
  • Permissions: Bunkr asks only for notifications and, if you turn on app lock, biometric unlock. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

9. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as the purpose in section 2 needs it, and then erase it.

  • Account, roster link, profile, contact details, attendance and syllabus progress: while your account exists. When you delete your account, or withdraw your consent, we erase them within 30 days.
  • Attendance marks you clear with "Clear all records" are wiped from your account straight away.
  • Contact details are removed the moment you delete them from your profile.
  • Leaderboard rank disappears as soon as you switch the leaderboard off or delete your account.
  • Roster entries of students who have not joined: until the end of the academic session, or until you ask us to remove yours.
  • Messages you send us: for one year after the matter is closed, unless a dispute or the law requires longer.
  • End of session: at the end of an academic session we may reset the section's data for the next one. We will tell users in the app at least 15 days before.

What the law makes us keep. Some records must be kept even after you delete your account, and we keep only these, only for the stated period, and only for the stated legal purpose:

  • the information you registered with (email address, username, roster serial number and the dates your account was opened and closed) for 180 days after your account is closed, as the Intermediary Rules, 2021 require;
  • security and access logs, and the data they relate to, for one year from the date of processing, where the DPDP Rules, 2025 or the directions of the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team require it;
  • anything a court, a lawful order or a live dispute requires us to preserve, for as long as that requirement lasts.

Provider logs and routine backups held by Supabase and Vercel are overwritten on those providers' own schedules. Data on your own device is removed when you sign out, clear site data or uninstall.

10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and the rules listed in section 1 you have the right to:

  • know and access: get a summary of the personal data we hold about you, what we do with it, and the names of everyone we have shared it with;
  • correct, complete and update anything that is wrong or out of date (most of it you can edit yourself in your profile);
  • erase: have your data deleted when it is no longer needed or when you withdraw consent;
  • withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawing does not make what we did before unlawful;
  • nominate another person to use these rights for you if you die or become unable to act;
  • complain to us, and if we do not resolve it, to the Data Protection Board of India (see Grievances and contact).

What you can do yourself, straight away: switch the leaderboard off in your profile (Section leaderboard → Hide me); delete any contact detail from your profile; clear every attendance mark with Attendance → Clear all records; sign out.

For everything else, email support@bunkr.website from the address on your account and include your Bunkr username, so we can be sure the request is yours. To nominate someone, send us their name, relationship to you and email address. We do not charge for any request. We acknowledge every request within 24 hours and complete it within 30 days. If we cannot do what you ask, we will tell you why, in writing, within that time.

Your part. The law also asks things of you: give us true details, claim only your own seat, do not pretend to be someone else, and do not file false or frivolous complaints.

11. Delete your account

Send a deletion request from the email address you signed up with, and include your Bunkr username. In the app the same request is under your profile: Delete account.

Request account deletion

Within 30 days we delete your sign-in account, your roster link, username and profile (contact details included), every attendance mark and total, your syllabus progress, your leaderboard entry, your device record and your cancelled-class reports, and we release your roster seat so it can be claimed again. You can delete part of your data without closing your account: clear all your attendance marks with Attendance → Clear all records, remove contact details in your profile, or switch the leaderboard off.

12. If you are under 18

Bunkr is made for college students. Under Indian law anyone under 18 is a child, and we may process a child's personal data only with the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian.

  • If you are under 18, do not create an account until your parent or guardian has read this policy and agreed to it for you. When you claim your seat you confirm that you are 18 or over, or that they have agreed.
  • We may ask the parent or guardian to confirm that consent directly. They do this by emailing support@bunkr.website from their own address with the student's name and username, their own full name and relationship to the student, a phone number we can verify them on, and a statement that they are an adult and consent. We keep that record for as long as the account exists.
  • A parent or guardian can see, correct or delete the student's data, or withdraw consent, at any time using the same address. When consent is withdrawn we delete the account.
  • If we learn that a student under 18 is using Bunkr without that consent, we will suspend the account and delete it if consent is not confirmed within 15 days.

We do not track or monitor the behaviour of any user, and we show no advertising of any kind, so no child is tracked, profiled or targeted with advertising on Bunkr. The same rule applies to a person with a disability who has a lawful guardian: the guardian's consent is needed and the guardian may use these rights.

13. Security and data breaches

We protect your data with reasonable security safeguards, including:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS) for every connection, and encrypted storage at our database provider;
  • passwords stored only as a salted hash by our sign-in provider;
  • row-level access rules in the database, so an account can read and change only its own private records, and the public views expose only the columns listed in section 5;
  • per-account limits on changes, and limits on repeated sign-in attempts;
  • the one-device rule, with a prompt when your account is active elsewhere;
  • access to the live database limited to us, and never placing enrolment numbers or other private data in the public source code;
  • logs at our providers that let unauthorised access be detected and investigated.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you without delay through your account or your registered email: what happened and when, what it may mean for you, what we have done about it, what you can do to protect yourself, and who to contact. We will also report it to the Data Protection Board of India without delay, with a full report within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and to the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team where its directions require.

You can help: use a password you use nowhere else, keep it to yourself, and tell us straight away if you think someone has used your account.
